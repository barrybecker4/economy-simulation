# Lab: Bitcoin

**Link:** `?lesson=bitcoin`

**Question:** What if nobody can print money?

## This lab cannot show

- This lab is one closed economy. There is no trade with other countries.
- A category price that rises does not mean people switched stores; it is an accounting split of one basket.
- The result depends on the lever you change. A different assumption can reverse the picture.
- This is one closed economy switching units, not two countries trading, and not the United States converting.

## 15-minute script

1. Open the link. Fiat is the pinned baseline.
2. Ask for a guess: if nobody can print money, what happens to prices versus fiat?
3. Switch the lever to Bitcoin, keep the guess, and press Run.
4. Compare the solid and dashed price and job lines.
5. Stress the cannot-show box: this is not a model of the US converting or of two trading countries.

## Student prompts

- Did prices end lower or higher under bitcoin?
- What happened to unemployment at the same time?

## Seed-1 answer key (120 months, registry population)

- Bitcoin versus fiat: CPI ends lower; unemployment ends higher.
- Matching guess: “Prices end lower than under fiat.”
